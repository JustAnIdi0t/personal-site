Professional

This page outlines professional work, areas of interest, and ongoing projects. The material here is intended to provide a clear, descriptive overview rather than a comprehensive or autobiographical account.

Areas of Work

  • Undergraduate academic study in psychology and communication studies, with an emphasis on how internal frameworks, perspectives, and communication shape individual experience
  • Interest in holistic approaches to health that consider psychological, social, and contextual dimensions of well-being
  • People-centered work focused on supporting individuals as they navigate cognitive, emotional, and situational aspects of their lives
  • Engagement with social advocacy and organizational efforts aimed at addressing structural inequities and supporting underrepresented voices

Current and Selected Projects

  • Design and development of a visual art exhibit at New Mexico State University exploring philosophical themes such as duality and non-duality, impermanence, and common misconceptions surrounding enlightenment and self-transformation
  • Ongoing work on a written piece examining related philosophical and cultural questions, intended for publication in The Agora, the New Mexico State University Honors College newspaper
  • Pursuit of people-centered professional roles that emphasize communication, support, and practical engagement in service-oriented or community-focused contexts
